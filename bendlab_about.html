About BendLab

BendLab asks a simple question with a measurable answer: when a sunflower seedling bends toward light, which of its internal settings does the bending curve actually reveal, and which stay hidden?

The question

We model a seedling with four settings: sensitivity (k), response lag (tau), a bend-rate cap (max_rate), and an effective light angle (phi0). If two very different settings produce the same curve, the curve cannot tell them apart, and any method that claims to read them off, from curve fitting to a neural network, is guessing. BendLab measures exactly where that information limit sits. It is a model-identifiability study, not yet evidence for a particular biological mechanism.

Key results

  • Degeneracy is real: different parameter combinations produce nearly identical bending curves.
  • One dial is blind: when the bend-rate cap never binds, max_rate cannot be recovered. Curve fits flag it in 47 of 48 measured plants; a 400,000-plant sweep and a trained neural network (about 44% error, versus 1 to 4% for the other dials) agree.
  • Fitting is not predicting: the model fits published curves well in-sample (median 1.80 degrees) but predicts held-out groups weakly (11.75 degrees).
  • A candidate fix for the worst failures: a shared circadian-clock term, fitted without the failing ZT9 group, improves all six held-out ZT9 plants (median 2.42 to 1.44 degrees). Promising, not confirmed.
  • Try it above: the Simulator shows the model forward, the Trained model reads curves at any observation hour, and the Curve fitter fits with its uncertainty warnings attached.

Limitations, in plain sight

  • The neural model has never seen a real plant. Fitted sunflower parameters often lie outside its training ranges, so do not apply it to measured curves.
  • One dataset, one species, one light quality underlies the population analyses.
  • Several earlier conclusions were corrected or withdrawn (a fitter time-base error, two hypothesis verdicts, one connectivity claim, one set of sample sizes). The corrections are documented in the repository, not buried.
  • Nothing here is a validated experimental protocol. The two-source design estimate (about 96% correct model selection at 20 seedlings per dataset) comes from a stated simulation procedure and awaits a laboratory.
